## Screening Intent

The primary bell should feel small, calm, close, clean, and slightly ritualistic.

Reject candidates that feel like:

- a UI notification
- a school bell
- a church/cathedral bell
- a fantasy sparkle that lacks physical bell weight
- a fast repeated jingle

Keep candidates that can support:

- a clean direction cue in quiet ambience
- a gentle location re-imprint when the button is pressed
- a soft resonance layer when the player holds or catches the bell
