## Design Intent

Rain should read as floor impact across a very wide, empty, flat plane.

Use two sound families:

- Wide rain bed: broad surface rainfall, not a sky source.
- Close droplets: local impacts that can imply drops hitting floor, bell, pad, shoulder, or near-body surfaces.

Avoid:

- thunder
- roof shelter
- obvious window glass
- forest canopy
- city ambience
- cafe or human presence
- cozy indoor rain
